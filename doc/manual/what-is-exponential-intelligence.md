# What is Exponential Intelligence, and what does it never do?

Exponential Intelligence is the AI assistant of the administration. It writes drafts of content, fills and improves
fields in the edit form, suggests alt text for images, and with Vector carries out bigger jobs that you review first.

## Who can do this

Everyone with an account in the administration can read this page. Using the assistant needs the policies described
in [Who may use it, and why don't I see it?](who-may-use-it.md)

## Steps

1. Find it in four places:
   - the **Intelligence** tab of any content in **Content structure**;
   - the **Exponential Intelligence** area at the top of the edit form, and the small buttons next to text fields;
   - **Dashboard** > **Exponential Intelligence**, the dashboard page with your usage and **Start writing**;
   - **Dashboard** > **EI: Vector**, the workspace for bigger jobs.
2. Know its three rules before you start:
   - it works **as you**: it can read only what you may read, and change only what you may change;
   - it **never publishes**: everything it writes is a draft or a value in the form;
   - every version it wrote carries the label **Exponential Intelligence**, in your drafts, in the version view and in
     the approval item.
3. Read and check what it writes, as you would check a colleague's draft.

## What can go wrong

- **It states something that is not true.** AI models can be wrong. Check facts, names, numbers and quotes before you
  publish. Where it lacks a fact it is told to write a placeholder such as [source needed].
- **It cannot set images, files or relations.** It says so; set them in the edit form yourself.
- **Content you give it contains instructions.** It treats content as data, not as orders, but review the result as
  you would any draft.
