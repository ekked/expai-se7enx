# How do I review the drafts it wrote and send them on?

Every draft the assistant wrote waits for you. You edit it, keep it as a draft, discard it, or send it for publishing,
where your site's approval workflow applies as for any other draft.

## Who can do this

The editor who asked for the drafts (`expai/write`). Approvers decide in **Collaboration** as for any other content.

## Steps

1. In the **Intelligence** tab, look at **Drafts written with Exponential Intelligence**. Each draft shows its state:
   Draft, Saved as draft, Waiting for approval or Published.
2. Open a draft with **View version** to read it, or **Edit** to change it in the normal edit form.
3. Remove a draft you do not want with **Discard** and confirm.
4. Tick the drafts that are ready.
5. Read the line above the buttons: "Waits for approval when sent", "May wait for approval when sent" or "Published at
   once when sent (no approval applies)".
6. Click **Save as drafts** to keep them (you find them in **My drafts**), or **Send for approval** to send them on.
   Confirm.
7. **Approvers:** open **Dashboard** > **Collaboration**. The item shows the label **Exponential Intelligence**.
   Approve or deny it as usual. Your waiting items are also listed under **Waiting for my approval** on the
   Exponential Intelligence dashboard.

## What can go wrong

- **"Send for approval" published at once.** No approval workflow covers that content, as the line above the buttons
  said.
- **The version stays pending.** The approval workflow waits for its cronjob or for the approver.
- **A denied draft.** It is your draft again; edit it and send it again.
