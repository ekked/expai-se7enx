# How do I ask the assistant to write or change content?

In the **Intelligence** tab of a folder or an article you write a request, and the assistant reads, plans and writes
drafts there, step by step, while you watch.

## Who can do this

Users whose role has `expai/write`. The assistant can only do what your own role allows at that place.

## Steps

1. Open the folder or article in **Content structure** and choose the **Intelligence** tab.
   Alternatively, use **Start writing** on **Dashboard** > **Exponential Intelligence**: write the request and click
   **Write**; the Intelligence tab of the chosen place opens with your request filled in.
2. Write what you want, for example "Write three short articles about our spring events under this folder, friendly
   tone, no images", or "Make the intro of this article shorter".
3. Click **Send** (or press Ctrl+Enter).
   Each step is shown in one line while it works. Steps that write are bold; a step that failed is red and says why.
4. Answer if it asks a question: when a request is unclear, it asks instead of guessing.
5. Look at **Drafts written with Exponential Intelligence** under the conversation, and open each draft with **Edit** or
   **View version**.
6. Continue in the same conversation to refine ("make the second one shorter"), or click **New conversation** to start
   over. Your drafts stay.

## What can go wrong

- **A step fails with "a field the class does not have" or "a place you may not create content in".** Ask more
  precisely, or ask an administrator about your rights there.
- **The answer is slow or times out.** Large requests take time. Split them, or use Vector for bulk work.
- **"The limit ... is reached".** A usage limit of you or the site is reached; it resets at the time shown.
