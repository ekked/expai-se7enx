# What does the AI send where?

The page **EI: Summary** answers this on one printable page: which providers are used, in which region, how long they
keep data, where the keys come from (never the keys themselves), who may use what, the limits, and the tools that
reach outside the site.

## Who can do this

Users whose role has `expai/summary`. It needs no other policy and no access to content, so it can be given to a data
protection officer's role.

## Steps

1. Open **Setup** > **EI: Summary** (or the link in the **Details** of the Exponential Intelligence dashboard).
2. Read the sections: master switch and surfaces, providers, lanes and their retention, data policy, limits and usage
   this month, tools that reach outside the site, what this site keeps, and the roles with Exponential Intelligence
   policies.
3. Print it, or save it as PDF from the browser, for your record of processing activities.

## What can go wrong

- **A provider shows no region or retention.** The provider's terms were not entered in the settings. Ask the
  administrator who set it up.
- **The contact line is empty.** Set who answers questions about the processing in the settings (`[SummarySettings]
  Contact`).
