# How do I fill or improve fields in the edit form?

While you edit, the assistant can fill all fields from a short description, or write, rewrite, shorten, translate or
suggest one field at a time. Nothing is saved until you store or publish the draft yourself.

## Who can do this

Users whose role has `expai/write`, editing content they may edit.

## Steps

1. Edit the content as usual.
2. To fill the whole form, open the **Exponential Intelligence** area at the top of the form with **Show**, describe what
   to write (for example "A short article about the opening of our new office, about 200 words") and click **Fill the
   fields**.
   The fields it filled are highlighted.
3. To work on one field, use the buttons next to it: **Write**, **Rewrite**, **Shorten**, **Translate** or **Suggest**.
4. For a one-click change, use the presets next to the fields your site set up (**Suggest title**, **Write summary**,
   **Meta description**, **Keywords**) or the **Improve** menu of a text field (Shorten, Expand, Clarify, Fix grammar,
   Simplify for new readers, More formal, More casual).
5. Read the new value. Click **Undo** to put the previous value back.
6. Click **Store draft** or **Send for publishing** as usual.

## What can go wrong

- **Nothing appears in a rich text field.** Reload the form and try again; if it persists, tell your administrator
  (see the troubleshooting page).
- **The value is in the wrong language.** Say the language in your instruction, or use **Translate**.
- **You want to know what a click costs.** The line **Defaults in use** above the fields names the model, where it runs
  and about what one click costs.
