# expai 0.0.1.12

No class or setting changes; templates and one script of the admin only.

## Updated

- Updated: The admin templates carry no inline JavaScript any more, so their pages work under a
  Content-Security-Policy without `'unsafe-inline'` in `script-src`. The "Where" form and "Choose all" of the missing
  alt text report and "Print or save as PDF" of EI: Summary are `data-exp-ai-*` attributes handled by the new
  `expai_page_actions.js`; the conversation form of the panel and the composer of Vector lose their
  `onsubmit="return false;"`, as their scripts already handle the submit. `tests/expaiinlinescripttest.php`
  (no database) keeps it that way.
