# expai 0.0.1.8

## Fixed

- Fixed: The bars of field actions are drawn with the edit form, so its fields no longer move down when expai.js
  starts (a layout shift of 0.047 at 1440 px in the admin4 designs). The bar comes from
  `content/edit/expai_attribute.tpl` through the kernel's content.ini `[EditSettings] AttributeTemplates[]`
  (Exponential 6.0.15 and later); its buttons are disabled until expai.js takes the bar on. On an older kernel, or a
  design without the hook, expai.js draws the bars itself as before.
- Fixed: Formulas, tables and embeds are no longer lost when Vector or a tool writes a rich text field. What the model
  cannot express in an ezxmltext field is sent as a placeholder and written back unchanged; a write that loses or
  invents a placeholder is refused (expAiRichTextTokens).

## Updated

- Updated: No field actions above a rich text field whose editor has an assistant of its own (Tiptap with its AI
  switched on); the presets of the field stay. `[EditViewSettings] HideActionsWithEditorAI=false` turns this off.
- Updated: The longest field value the edit form sends is a setting, `[EditViewSettings] MaxFieldLength` (default
  20000, 1000 to 200000). A longer field is cut when it is sent, so its answer is shown for review instead of
  replacing the whole field.
